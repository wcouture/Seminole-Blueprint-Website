Scanning

A significant trend in the construction industry over the past few years has been the demand for digitizing construction documents, from initial bid documents to completed as-built construction information. Most architects, engineers, contractors and public agencies have document storage areas where decades of hard-copy records are managed for generations of buildings and renovations. Maintaining paper archives is labor-intensive and difficult. In addition, storing hard copies presents the risk of loss from fire, flood and mismanagement. Working with Seminole Blueprint, we can offer a 21st Century digital solution for your storage needs. Scanning can greatly reduce the operational cost of running your firm by allowing you to convert storage space into revenue generating office space.
