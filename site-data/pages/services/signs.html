Mounting and Laminations

SemBlueInc offers complete mounting and lamination services so you can protect your documents and projects. Using specialized heavy rolling equipment and vacuum press, we attach your documents to a substrate (a rigid backing) with a perfectly flat, bubble free finish.

Add a clean finish to your prints with our lamination service. Laminated prints are encapsulated with a UV-protected polyester film (also known as "Mylar") in glossy, luster, and matte finishes. Lamination can be a great complement to digital printing that will protect your prints from sunlight, fingerprints, pollution, or moisture for years to come.
